“AI slop” is a term often used to describe the flood of low-quality, mass-produced content generated by artificial intelligence. It usually refers to text, images, or videos that lack originality, depth, or human nuance—produced quickly and cheaply to maximize clicks, fill websites, or generate ad revenue. Because AI models can churn out vast amounts of material with minimal effort, entire platforms can become saturated with repetitive, shallow, or misleading content. This phenomenon raises concerns about the dilution of valuable human creativity, the spread of misinformation, and the difficulty of distinguishing genuine, thoughtful work from automated filler. Ultimately, AI slop highlights the tension between technological efficiency and cultural or informational quality.
